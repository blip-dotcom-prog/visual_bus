Other reviews to expect

Historic review (HDLC): the city's GIS puts this lot in the Faubourg Marigny local historic district, Full Control. New construction, demolition and exterior changes need HDLC approval.
Curb cuts: every driveway option needs two new curb cuts across the sidewalk. New or widened curb cuts need city permits, and with the HDLC and the EC design overlay on this lot, expect them to be reviewed too.
Buses on site: zoning above covers buildings and land use. Using buses as lodging, a bar, food vending or storage is permitted separately — check with Safety & Permits before you bid. The CZO also requires most activity on a lot to be indoors, with listed exceptions (Sec. 21.5).
Overlays on this parcel (city GIS): RDO-1 Residential Diversity Overlay · EC Enhancement Corridor Design Overlay (design review) · Transient Lodging Interim Zoning District · Data Center Interim Zoning District.
Behind & beside: no alley. The rear line is shared with 1031 Frenchmen St. (Sq. 374 Lot 21, 28′ × 112′, a double), which runs behind both this lot and 2112 St. Claude. The east side line is shared with 2112 St. Claude (28′ × 56′, a bar building). Both are also zoned HMC-2. Easements or servitudes don't show on city maps, so ask for the title work and a survey.
